# Code of conduct

This is a small, personal project. The standard is simple: be respectful and be useful.

- Assume good faith, and disagree about ideas, not people.
- Keep reports and reviews specific: say what you ran, what happened and what you expected.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing someone else's private information.
- No sharing real secrets, tokens or private code in issues, pull requests or discussions. Redact them first.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who do not follow this. This applies to every space the project uses: issues, pull requests, discussions and the repository itself.

To report a conduct problem privately, open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/sairam0424/nh-skills/security/advisories/new) and say it is a conduct report, or open an issue titled only `Conduct concern` with no details and the maintainer will arrange a private channel. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
